A small release with no change to the app. It adds a research study to the repository: a sweep of the built-in strategy families over the top crypto, stocks and indices, looking for strategies that win 75% of trades at R:R 1 or 50% at R:R 4, with a hold-out check and a shuffled-returns baseline.

## Highlights

- **Under the hood**: a new strategy search study in `docs/spikes/strategy-search/` (the probe script, the symbol universe, the full results and a write-up of what survives on unseen data and against shuffled-return copies), linked from `docs/STRATEGIES.md`. Nothing in the app, the server or the build changed.

## Upgrade notes

- **Nothing to do.** No change to the app, saved data, the API, the dependencies, Docker or deploy config. Upgrading from v0.15.0 and downgrading back are both safe.

To deploy, build from the tag (`git checkout v0.15.1`, then `docker compose up -d --build`); no registry image of the app is published.

## Changes

- Strategy search spike: sweep top crypto, stocks and indices for 75%/R:R 1 and 50%/R:R 4 strategies, with hold-out and shuffled baseline (#414)
